Crystal Structure

Cell and amplitude damping

Every atom's amplitude is multiplied by exp(−B·s²), with s = sinθ/λ. B adds two effects: the finite size of the atom (form factor f(s) ≈ f0·exp(−Bfs²)) and the thermal motion (Debye–Waller, BT = 8π²⟨u²⟩): B = Bf + BT. B = 0 gives point atoms with a constant f.
